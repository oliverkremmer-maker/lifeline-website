Subscriptions

Cancel a subscription: Settings (on your iPhone or iPad) → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Lifeline OS → Cancel Subscription.

Free trial: Cancel any time before day 7 to avoid being charged.

Restore purchases: Open Lifeline OS → Settings → Restore purchases.

Refunds: Refund requests for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com while signed in to the Apple ID used for the purchase.

Common questions

The paywall shows "Loading prices…" forever. Tap "Tap to retry". If it persists, check your network connection or sign in to the App Store.

Notifications aren't showing. Settings → Notifications → Lifeline OS → enable. In the app, Settings → Notifications → re-toggle the brief you want.

I want to start over. In the app: Settings → Erase all data. This cannot be undone — export your Brain first if you want a backup.

Family Sharing. Lifeline Pro (monthly and annual) is shareable with your Family Sharing group. Lifeline Plus is not.
