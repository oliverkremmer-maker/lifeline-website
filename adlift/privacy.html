AddLift

Privacy Policy

Last updated May 2026 · Effective upon App Store approval

This is a plain-language privacy policy for AddLift, an iOS app published by Oliver Kremmer. It explains what data the app handles and what we do (and don't do) with it. Kept short on purpose.

TL;DR
  • AddLift runs locally on your device.
  • We don't have a server. We don't have your data.
  • The app calls OpenAI directly using your own API key, stored in your iCloud Keychain.
  • The only personal info Apple shares with us is which subscription tier you're on.

What we collect

Nothing on our servers, because we don't have any. Everything AddLift generates — your prompts, ad copy, image and video assets, brand kits — stays on your device, in the app's local storage. Never uploaded to us.

When you make a purchase, Apple shares a single fact: which subscription product you bought (Spark, Creator, Studio, or Agency). That's it. We don't see your name, email, payment details, or Apple ID.

What we don't collect

  • We don't track you.
  • We don't run analytics.
  • We don't have crash reporting that includes your content.
  • We don't sell your data — we couldn't, because we don't have it.
  • We don't have advertisers, partners, or third-party SDKs that collect anything.

Your OpenAI API key

To generate ads, you provide your own OpenAI API key in Settings. That key is stored in your iCloud Keychain on your device. iCloud Keychain is end-to-end encrypted — even Apple can't read it.

When the app generates an ad, it sends your prompt directly to OpenAI's API using your key. OpenAI's privacy policy applies — see openai.com/privacy. We are not in the loop.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple via In-App Purchase. Apple handles all payment information. We never see your credit card, Apple ID, billing address, or payment details.

We receive a token from Apple's StoreKit framework that tells us which subscription tier you're on so the app can unlock the right features. That token contains no personal information.

Children

AddLift is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect any information from anyone under 13.

Your rights

Because we don't collect data on our servers, there's nothing for us to delete on request — your data lives on your device. To delete everything: Settings → Danger zone → Delete all generated ads. Or uninstall the app.

If you live in the EU/UK and want to exercise GDPR rights (access, deletion, etc.), email us — but in most cases the answer will be: we don't have any data on you to access or delete.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated in-app.
